## 1003 L298N: Electronics Lab Experiment Plan
- STEM Starter Kit Series, Part 4, supporting lab outline (planning draft)

###Plan and Design Rationale
- This document lays out ten experiments (labs) built entirely from the six items labeled in the Electronics Lab
photograph: the Arduino Uno, the standalone ATmega328P, the L298N motor driver, the LS7366R quadrature
counter, the Cytron 12VDC geared motors with encoders, and the thumb joystick. It expands the four example
labs already sketched, I2C wiring, MCU-to-L298N wiring, joystick wiring, and joystick programming, into a full
build sequence that touches every component in the photograph. Nothing in Article 1003 has been changed;
this is a planning outline only, meant to be checked against your other sources before it is written into the
article.
